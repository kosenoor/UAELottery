UAE Lottery Investment Sharing Agreement

This agreement is made between the following participants for jointly purchasing UAE lottery tickets on a shared investment basis:

Noor, Dhinesh, Mani, Nikah, Stan, Rohan, Sowmya

  1. Each participant shall contribute AED 50 per month toward the lottery investment fund.
  2. Lottery tickets will be purchased weekly using the collected amount. The number of weekly purchases may be 4 or 5 times per month depending on the number of Fridays in that month.
  3. Any remaining balance (AED 50 or AED 100, if applicable) will be used for an additional ticket purchase at the end of the month, regardless of the day it falls on.
  4. Accordingly, the total monthly investment will be AED 350.
  5. The responsibility for purchasing and managing the lottery investment will rotate every month among the participants.
  6. The assigned participant for that month will handle collection, purchase, sharing proof, and maintaining transparency.
  7. After completion of one month, responsibility transfers to the next participant in rotation order.
  8. All lottery tickets are jointly owned by all listed members.
  9. Any prize amount will be shared equally among all members unless mutually agreed otherwise in writing before purchase.
  10. Lottery participation involves risk and no guaranteed winnings exist.
  11. Remaining minor balances may be carried forward or used for extra tickets based on mutual agreement.
  12. Jackpot or major winnings will be distributed after official receipt of funds.
  13. Disputes will be resolved mutually in a fair and respectful manner.
  14. No participant can claim exclusive ownership of any ticket purchased from shared funds.
  15. New members can be added only with unanimous agreement of existing participants.
  16. Any participant leaving must inform others before next monthly cycle.
  17. This agreement is based on mutual trust, transparency, and equal sharing among all participants.
